The Story of Chia Cafe

Our Story

From a Simple Idea to a Cozy Space

Chia Cafe is a restaurant-cum-café in Avanti Vihar, Raipur, created around a simple idea: good food becomes even better when it is shared with good people.

Chia Cafe brings together the relaxed feeling of a café and the variety of a restaurant. Our menu moves across familiar favourites - South Indian, North Indian, Chinese and café-style food - giving guests plenty of reasons to visit and return.

We believe people visit a café for different reasons. Sometimes it is about the food. Sometimes it is about the coffee. Sometimes it is simply about having a comfortable place to meet someone.

That is the experience we want to create: a place where you can come for a meal, stay for the conversation and leave with a good memory.
