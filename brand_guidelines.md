# Chia Cafe - Brand Guidelines & Digital Marketing Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
- **Brand Name:** Chia Cafe
- **Location:** Avanti Vihar, Raipur
- **Vibe & Concept:** A premium cafe with a natural, organic, and welcoming atmosphere. The branding reflects a focus on healthy, fresh ingredients (like chia) paired with a modern, earthy aesthetic.

## 2. Visual Identity & Design System

### Color Palette
- **Primary Dark Green:** `#0E4935` (Used for main headings, primary buttons, and strong brand presence)
- **Accent Light Green:** `#84A57B` (Used for button hovers, highlights, and secondary elements)
- **Soft Mint/Pastel Green:** `#C5D9B2` (Used for borders, subtle backgrounds, and light text)
- **Earthy Sand/Beige:** `#EBE5DB` (Used for section backgrounds to give a warm, organic feel)
- **Deep Forest/Black:** `#0A2318` (Used for footer backgrounds and high-contrast text)

### Typography
- **Headings (H1 - H6):** `Fredoka`, sans-serif. This provides a friendly, rounded, and premium look.
- **Body Text:** `Open Sans`, sans-serif. Highly readable, clean, and modern for paragraphs and UI elements.

## 3. Social Media & Digital Marketing Guidelines

### Graphic Design Instructions (For Canva/Photoshop)
- **Imagery:** Use high-quality, warm, and natural photography. Incorporate elements of nature, coffee beans, fresh food, and cozy cafe interiors.
- **Layout:** Keep it clean and uncluttered. Use `#EBE5DB` (Sand) for background canvases, with text in `#0E4935` (Dark Green).
- **Graphics/Shapes:** Use soft, rounded shapes and organic lines rather than sharp edges, mirroring the 'Fredoka' font style.
- **Buttons/Call to Action:** Make CTAs pop using `#0E4935` and `#84A57B` for hover/secondary states.

### Content Strategy (Instagram & Facebook)
- **Tone of Voice:** Welcoming, organic, premium, and community-focused. 
- **Post Pillars:**
  1. **Food & Drink Highlights:** Showcasing signature dishes and drinks with a focus on freshness.
  2. **Atmosphere/Vibe:** Highlighting the cozy, natural interior of the Avanti Vihar location.
  3. **Behind the Scenes:** The making of coffee, fresh ingredients, and staff interactions.
  4. **Community/Customer Love:** Reposting customer stories, testimonials, and local Raipur events.
